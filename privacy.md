# Privacy Policy

QR Password Exchanger is designed to work without user accounts, cloud services, or central servers.

The application does not collect personal information, analytics data, advertising identifiers, or usage statistics.

Passwords, recovery codes, encryption keys, and other secrets are processed locally on the user's device. The developer does not receive, store, or have access to this information.

Encrypted messages may be shared through third-party applications chosen by the user (for example email or messaging apps). Any data shared through such services is subject to the privacy policies of those providers.

As a beta version, the application may contain software defects. Users should evaluate the software before using it for highly sensitive information.
