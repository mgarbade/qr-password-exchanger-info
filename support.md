# Support

## What does the app do?

QR Password Exchanger allows users to securely exchange passwords and other secrets using public-key cryptography.

## Does the app require an internet connection?

No.

Encryption and decryption work locally on the device.

## Does the app require an account?

No.

## Can I use WhatsApp, Signal, Teams, or Email?

Yes.

The recommended workflow is:

1. Exchange public keys once.
2. Encrypt the secret.
3. Send the encrypted message through your preferred communication channel.

Only the intended recipient can decrypt it.

## Can the developer recover my secrets?

No.

The developer has no access to your private keys or encrypted messages.

## I lost my device. Can my secrets be recovered?

Possibly not.

Users are responsible for maintaining access to their devices and cryptographic keys.
